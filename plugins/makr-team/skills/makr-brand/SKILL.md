# Makr: the brand brief

Every Makr agent works from this brief. If something here conflicts with a newer instruction from Brennan, follow Brennan and flag the conflict so this file can be updated.

## Who Makr is
- **Makr** (never "Makr.ai"; gomakr.ai is only the domain) is a practical growth and automation partner for **owner-led businesses**, typically 5–100 employees.
- Founder-led: **Brennan Burks**, Westford, Massachusetts. 13 years leading communications and marketing in manufacturing, financial services, biotech and higher education. He is personally accountable for every engagement.
- Based in New England: Massachusetts, southern New Hampshire, and remote across New England.
- Central promise: Makr builds the digital systems that help growing businesses **get found, capture more opportunities and operate with less manual work**. AI is a capability, not the product.
- Homepage line: **"Win more work. Waste less time."**

## The offer: four connected services
1. **Website Development & Management**: design, development, hosting, maintenance, landing pages, conversion improvements, integrations.
2. **Search Visibility & Content Marketing**: local and technical SEO, AEO (answer engine optimization), GEO (visibility in AI answers), Google Business Profile, industry content, digital marketing.
3. **Lead Follow-Up & Revenue Recovery**: missed-call text back, instant inquiry response, estimate and proposal follow-up, reminders, reactivation, review requests.
4. **AI Workflow Automation**: intake and routing, quoting support, document processing, customer communication drafts, internal reporting.

The single next step everywhere is **book a free Growth Gap Review** (button text: "Find Your Growth Gaps"). Prices are public and fixed. Details: [services-and-pricing.md](services-and-pricing.md).

## Who we sell to
Five markets: home services (trades), professional services (law, accounting), personal services (dental, medical, vet, med spa), manufacturing, B2B services. What each owner cares about and how their customers judge them: [markets.md](markets.md).

## How we sound and what we never say
Direct, practical, plain English, no hype. Outcomes first, features second. The non-negotiable rules on claims, statistics and AI language are in [voice-and-claims.md](voice-and-claims.md). Read it before writing anything public.
