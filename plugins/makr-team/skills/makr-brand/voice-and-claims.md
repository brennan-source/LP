# Voice and claim rules

## Voice
- Direct, contractor-friendly, no hype. Write like a trusted advisor who charges real money.
- Outcome first, then how. Short sentences, active voice, plain words.
- Say "owners," "customers," "jobs," "patients," "clients," "RFQs": the buyer's words, not marketing words.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "We build AI" | "We help you win more work" |
| "AI-powered workflows" | "Save hours every week" |
| "We automate businesses" | "We take repetitive office work off your team" |
| "AI-powered X" | "X" or "automated X" |
| "Leverage," "synergy," "unlock," "game-changer" | Say what actually happens |

## Claims: hard rules
1. **No invented results.** Makr has no published client case studies or testimonials yet. Never write client results, percentages, testimonials or reviews as if they were real.
2. **Samples are labeled.** Any example business, number or report must be clearly marked as a sample for a fictional business.
3. **No guarantees** of rankings, AI mentions, lead volume or revenue.
4. **Statistics need a source.** Every number about the market needs a cited source and an honest strength rating (see the research skill). Vendor-sponsored figures are directional only and must be presented that way.
5. **No fake familiarity.** In outreach, never imply a relationship, referral or past conversation that didn't happen.
6. **Only real offers.** Prices and terms come only from the services-and-pricing table.

If a request would break one of these rules, say so and offer a version that doesn't.
