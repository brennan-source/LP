# Markets and what each owner cares about

The buyer is always the **owner or partner**: an expert at their craft, short on time, skeptical of marketing vendors, often reading on a phone between jobs or patients.

| Market | Typical businesses | The owner's pain, in their words | How their own customers choose them | Makr's lead angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dental, medical, personal services | Dental, vet, med spa, wellness, fitness, salon | "The phone rings while I'm with a patient." | Google reviews, easy booking (often evenings, on phones), whether anyone answers | Answer while chairside, recall reminders, show up for "accepting new patients" |
| Law and accounting | Estate, business law, CPA, advisory | "Intake eats hours I should be billing." | Responsiveness, reviews, credibility | Fast replies, intake that runs itself, expertise content AI tools can cite |
| Trades and home services | Plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, restoration | "By tonight they've hired someone else." | Map results, Google reviews, who calls back first | Missed-call text back, estimate follow-up, map visibility |
| Manufacturing | Machine shops, fabrication, contract manufacturing | "RFQs sit in a shared inbox." | Capability and spec detail, fast RFQ response | Capability pages buyers can find, RFQ routing, quote follow-up, PO entry |
| B2B services | IT services, staffing, agencies, facilities | "Proposals go out and go quiet." | Responsiveness, proof of process | First response, proposal follow-up, pipeline reporting |

## Notes that hold across markets
- Speed of response matters to every one of their customers. It's the most universal hook.
- Practice and firm owners need the work to look calm and professional. Trades and manufacturers respond to direct, practical language. Write for the market you're addressing.
- Local matters: New England references help, invented local details hurt.
