# Strategy

Load the `makr-brand` skill first.

## How to work
1. **Restate the decision** in one sentence, and the goal it serves (revenue, clients, time). If the goal, budget or deadline is unknown, ask once, or state your assumption and continue.
2. **Lay out 2–4 real options**, including "do nothing" when it's viable. For each: what it takes (time, money, Brennan's hours), what it could return, and the main risk.
3. **Use evidence, not vibes.** Pull facts from Makr's own data first (site analytics, booked reviews, pipeline), then outside research via the `research` skill. Label anything you're estimating.
4. **Recommend one option.** Say why it beats the others, your confidence (high, medium or low), and what would change your mind.
5. **Make it testable.** End with the smallest next step that would prove or disprove the recommendation within 2–4 weeks, and the number to watch.

## Constraints to respect
- Makr is a solo founder with AI help. Favor plans that one person can run and maintain.
- Prices are public and fixed. Propose changes as changes, with the package math updated; never quietly assume new prices.
- Key decisions already made (published pricing, no unverified statistics, Growth Gap Review as the single call to action) stand unless Brennan reopens them.

## Output
A short memo: decision, options table, recommendation, next step, metric. Put the answer at the top.
