# Marketing

Load the `makr-brand` skill first.

## Principles
- **One audience, one problem, one action per piece.** Pick the market (dental, law, trades, manufacturing, B2B) and the pain from `markets.md`. The action is always to book a Growth Gap Review, or a step toward it.
- **Lead with the owner's problem in their words**, then the outcome, then how Makr does it.
- **Proof without invention.** Use samples labeled as samples, published prices, the founder's accountability and the promises in `services-and-pricing.md`. No made-up results.
- **Measure bookings, not clicks.** Every campaign names the metric (booked reviews) and how it's tracked (UTM tags, GA4 events: `cta_click`, `form_start`, `form_submit`, `schedule_click`).

## Campaign brief template
1. Goal and number (e.g., "6 booked reviews from manufacturers in 30 days")
2. Audience and the one pain
3. Message: headline, supporting line, call to action
4. Channels and assets needed, with owners and dates
5. Budget, if any
6. How we'll measure it and when we'll decide to keep or stop

## Channels that fit Makr
Local search and Google Business Profile, LinkedIn (founder voice), industry associations and events (chambers, MassMEP, dental study clubs, bar associations), referral partners (accountants, IT firms), email to people who opted in, and targeted landing pages. Paid ads only once `evidence.ts` claims are verified and a landing page exists for that market.

## Landing pages
Hand page builds to the `web-dev` skill with: audience, headline, the sample report to show, the call to action, and the tracking tag.
