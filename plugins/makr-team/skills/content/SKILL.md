# Content

Load the `makr-brand` skill first, especially `voice-and-claims.md`.

## Before writing
- **Who is it for?** One market and one owner question. Write the question down in the owner's words. That question is often the title.
- **What should they do next?** Usually book a Business Growth Assessment, or read the related service page.
- **What do we know is true?** Gather facts with the `research` skill, and keep the sources.

## Writing rules
- Answer the question in the first two sentences. Then explain.
- Short paragraphs, descriptive subheadings, plain language. One idea per paragraph.
- Use specific, local, practical examples. Label fictional examples as fictional.
- Cite statistics with links and keep vendor figures framed as directional.
- No hype words, no "AI-powered," and no promises of rankings or results.

## Structure for search and AI answers (SEO, AEO, GEO)
- Title and H1 match the real question an owner would type or ask an AI assistant.
- A short, direct answer near the top that could stand alone as a quoted answer.
- An FAQ section with 3–6 real follow-up questions, each answered in 2–4 sentences.
- Mention the service area (Massachusetts, southern New Hampshire, New England) where it's natural.
- Suggest internal links to the matching service and industry pages, and a meta description under 155 characters.

## Formats
- **Article or guide:** 800–1,500 words, with FAQ.
- **Industry page copy:** pains, how the four services apply, sample week. Hand it to `web-dev` to build.
- **LinkedIn post (founder voice):** first person, one observation from real work, one practical takeaway, no hashtags pile-up.
- **Newsletter:** one useful idea, one link, one call to action.

## Output
A draft, plus: target question, meta description, suggested internal links, and sources. Brennan approves before anything is published.
